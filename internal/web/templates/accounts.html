2. Connect Google

The default scope is gmail.readonly. That is enough to read new mail. Labels that ghook applies, and Gmail filters, need extra scopes. Turn those on only if you want them, then connect again.

Reconnect after changing scopes. Google shows an unverified-app screen for a personal Gmail account; that is expected until you publish the consent screen.

3. Pub/Sub

Pull is the default: ghook subscribes from inside your network. A service account with Pub/Sub Subscriber on the subscription is the robust choice, because it does not depend on the Gmail refresh token. The OAuth grant above is the alternative when that Google user can manage the topic.

Push endpoint: {{.PushURL}}. Leave the audience empty to use that URL. Optional service-account email to require in the JWT:

Create uses the saved credentials. Grant adds roles/pubsub.publisher for gmail-api-push@system.gserviceaccount.com. Recommended pull settings: ack deadline 60s, retention 7 days, expiration never.
